A little more context

Good to know.

Can I use my own motion?

The connected lab studio accepts compatible G1 motion files: an NPZ containing poses and frame rate, or a supported G1 CSV. This public site lets you explore a selection of recorded runs. It does not run the model or accept uploads. A supplied motion can produce several passing scenes, or none.

Use Upload in the motion controls to supply a compatible G1 NPZ or CSV. The format and length requirements appear there. Generation runs on the connected server, and a supplied motion can produce several passing scenes, or none.

What makes a pair pass?

The exact geometry check measures clearance between the robot's collision capsules and the generated boxes. It keeps scenes where the input motion clears the obstacles by the requested margin and the tested alternative movements are blocked. It does not prove the movement is the only possible solution.

Is this the robot executing a policy?

This is recorded motion and scene generation, shown as a kinematic replay. Tracking the motion with a controller in physics, or on a real robot, requires separate validation. Deploy Studio explores the planning and execution side.

What does the download include?

Export scene JSON saves the selected scene's obstacle geometry, motion identifier, generator settings and check results. The motion's pose sequence is not included in that download. Rejected candidates keep their failed check in the export.
