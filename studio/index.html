About

SceneFlow is the learned motion-to-scene generator of Motion2Scene Flow. Given only a humanoid motion, it draws obstacle scenes in which that motion is necessary: the motion clears every box by a margin, and the walk it could have done instead runs into one. An exact check on the robot's collision capsules decides which draws pass.

The generator runs live on our lab machine, one request at a time, with a small queue and per-visitor limits. Uploaded files are read in memory and deleted right away; nothing you upload is stored. When the machine is busy or off, the recorded runs still play.

Every run here was generated by SceneFlow on our lab machine and recorded in full. The page replays each of the 40 denoising steps, the snap that slides each box toward the body, the exact check, and the motion walking through the chosen scene. It runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

Robot model: Unitree G1 (BSD-3). Motions: Kimodo text-to-motion and SONIC tracker continuations.
