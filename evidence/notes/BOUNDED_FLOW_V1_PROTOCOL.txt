Bounded proxy flow v1 — protocol before training and new test predictions

Question: can output parameterization fix the observed domain violations while
retaining geometric feasibility, coverage and candidate diversity? Is finite-step
integration a sufficient explanation of the Euclidean model's violations?

This adaptive follow-up uses the same v1 training/validation scenes, 96-control pool,
frozen reconstruction encoder, and validation-selected local attention conditioner.
The published local-FM checkpoint from proxy-flow-v0 is the frozen Euclidean
baseline. Draw 48 fresh test families (144 scenes), seed 20260923, excluding prior
test draws. Generate the identical motion parameter pool with seed262209 and bind
its content. No test-based model, threshold, transform or solver selection.

Compare frozen Euclidean FM with a newly trained tanh-chart FM. Both receive the
same 44 conditioning scalars, training scenes/motion IDs, architecture, optimizer
budget, initialization, and Gaussian/time draws. Only the target coordinates change.
Euclidean targets are physical controls normalized to [-1,1]. For bounded targets,
clip normalized demonstration endpoints only to [-1+delta,1-delta], delta=1e-4,
apply atanh, then center/scale each coordinate using the exact training sampling
distribution (uniform feasible scene, uniform valid motion within that scene).
Decoder is tanh(mu+sigma*y), followed by affine conversion to physical controls.
This bounds finite decoded outputs by construction; it is not learned collision
safety. Whitening changes the optimization geometry as part of this recipe.

The endpoint inset moves an exact domain endpoint by at most 0.0000225 m of
crouch, 0.0000785 rad of yaw, or 0.000025 m of lateral displacement (up to float32
rounding). Recheck inset targets using geometric labels, record all validity flips
and maximum coordinate changes, and retain the original training selection. If
flips exist, disclose that some transformed targets no longer meet the old labels;
do not silently change examples. Preserve representability of interior points.

Include terminal clipping of the frozen Euclidean Euler16 output as a baseline.
This is a declared, separately evaluated intervention, not a hidden repair of the
old results. Recompute complete geometry after clipping; parameters in range alone
do not establish feasibility. Do not apply extra clipping to tanh outputs.

For each fitted seed and both parameterizations, evaluate Euler16, Euler64,
Euler256, and Heun128 (256 vector-field evaluations). All solvers start from the
same per-seed Gaussian samples; no retraining for solver changes. Euler16 and
terminal clipping are the equal-sampling-compute primary comparisons. Higher-step
conditions diagnose convergence, not equal-latency performance. Treat Heun128 as
a numerical reference only; it is not an exact ODE solution or error certificate.
Record endpoint RMS differences in normalized physical controls and changes in
domain membership against that reference, alongside domain violation rates.

Keep 2,000 Adam updates, batch128, hidden128, lr .001, final checkpoint, three seeds.
Each method has 32 candidates per scene and the same exact swept-sphere/AABB
checker and proxy cost: crouch+.1*yaw+.05*abs(shift). Repeat fixed32 retrieval and
uniform32 sampling on this new test set as contextual baselines. Repeated retrieval
rows are one deterministic bank, not independent fits.

Primary: fraction of candidates satisfying both parameter-domain and >=20 mm
clearance constraints. Report scene coverage (at least one valid /32), collision
and insufficient-margin rejection separately, out-of-domain rates, selected cost
with selection count, and paired selected costs on jointly successful scenes.
Report mean pairwise normalized-control distance among valid candidates within
scenes with >=2 valid candidates, the number of such scenes, near-boundary mass
(within1% of any physical parameter range) among valid candidates, and minimum
clearance distributions for valid candidates. These are descriptive diagnostics;
do not equate parameter diversity with useful task/skill diversity.

Predeclared paired family-bootstrap contrasts: tanh/Euler16 minus
Euclidean/Euler16; tanh/Euler16 minus clipped/Euclidean16; and Euclidean/Euler256
minus Euclidean/Euler16. Report differences for raw validity and scene coverage,
resample 48 scene families with all descendant scenes, average the three fitted
seed differences, 2,000 draws. Intervals condition on fitted models, are exploratory
and unadjusted for multiple comparisons; they do not cover acquisition or seed
population uncertainty. Cost comparisons use paired common-success scenes and
report denominators; overall cost averages may involve different scenes.

The corpus and models remain a three-control, fixed-goal, oracle-scene sphere proxy.
No MTC training, human joint generation, partial-observation navigation, balance,
SONIC execution or real robot safety is established. A favorable bounds result is
grounds for the next interface experiment, not evidence of full-motion transfer.
