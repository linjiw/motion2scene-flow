Spatial readout v3 and conditional proxy-flow v0 — before results

Both stages are exploratory continuations. Preserve earlier runs. No robot dynamics,
MTC training, sensory occlusion, native SONIC tokens or new method novelty is claimed.

Stage A: spatial reading. Keep v1 training/validation and frozen reconstruction
encoders, draw 48 fresh test families with seed 20260921. No old test geometry is
used. For each swept sphere segment, sample 17 positions and expose the same 8
surrounding grid vertices per position: 136 scalar SDF reads, before deduplication.
Learned tokens contain SDF, vertex displacement from the segment midpoint, and
sample time along the segment. Query context contains endpoints, radius and the
physical segment index. The 1,400-scalar privileged grid remains available for
indexed access: this is not a 32-scalar-only compression comparison.

Compare uniform pooling and dot-product attention with the same token/value/query
networks, 32-dimensional features, head, initialization, updates and sampled labels.
The uniform arm computes the same logits but gives them zero weight; the unused
key/query gradient is a consequence of removing learned weighting, not a parameter
budget gain. Attention without XYZ offsets is a separately trained ablation.
Mean pooling is a fixed spatial-sample aggregation baseline, not trilinear
interpolation. The deterministic trilinear 17-point readout is a separate reference.
There is no deterministic clearance residual bypass in the learned arms.

Train 1,000 steps per arm/seed, 12 scenes x 32 queries, MSE on normalized clipped
clearance, half uniform and half near boundary as before. Test 3 seeds. Compare at
10/20/30/40/50% ranked candidate coverage; primary unsafe/accepted at 30%. Report
fixed 20 mm prediction threshold results as well. Retest the archived v2 global
reconstruction/MSE readout on the fresh test set. Primary planned contrasts:
attention minus uniform; no-XYZ attention minus attention; uniform minus archived
global. Paired family bootstrap, 2,000 draws, rerank each sample, fixed fitted seeds;
unadjusted exploratory intervals. No test-driven model/step selection.

Use validation results ONLY to choose a conditioning reader for stage B: among
uniform and attention, select the arm with lower seed-mean risk at 30% coverage,
tie broken toward uniform, provided risk <=5%. Otherwise use the deterministic
local-grid reference. No-XYZ is diagnostic, not a selection candidate. Record this
choice and qualification source before stage B training. Passing this ranking gate
does not qualify a reader as a deployable safety filter.

Stage B: minimal conditional flow-matching experiment. Draw another 48 fresh test
families with seed 20260922, keeping v1 train/validation. Construct one scene-
independent bank of 96 motion parameters: the original 12 plus uniform continuous
samples with seed 262209. Parameter ranges: crouch [0,.45] m, yaw [0,1.57] rad,
lateral shift [-.25,.25] m. Decode with the existing proxy body profile and 21 knots.
These are three continuous trajectory controls, not full joint trajectories or
arbitrary discrete token IDs. Navigation goal and traversal direction are fixed.

Label the pool with analytic swept-sphere/AABB geometry. For training, sample a
feasible training scene uniformly, then a geometrically valid pool motion uniformly.
Report excluded training scenes; do not train on test labels. The pool is an oracle
geometric demonstration construction, not human data or measured executed behavior.
The parameter pool is shared across splits, so novel motion/skill transfer is not
established. At inference, flow samples are continuous, not selected pool IDs.

Scale parameter domains to [-1,1], use epsilon ~ N(0,I), uniform transport time t,
x_t=(1-t)epsilon+t*x_1, squared velocity loss against x_1-epsilon. Identical 128-wide
MLPs and seeds compare frozen 32-dimensional global context versus that context
plus 12 local compatibility scores for the fixed anchor motions. In the global arm
the extra 12 inputs are zero; both arms have the same architecture. The local arm
has additional geometric access, which must be disclosed. Anchor queries are the
same during training/inference, avoiding clean-future-based feature selection.
This is fixed-anchor conditioning, not noisy-trajectory attention or a tested motion
codec. 2,000 Adam updates, batch128, lr .001, final checkpoint; 16-step Euler ODE.

Generate 32 candidates per scene for each method. Reject out-of-domain generated
controls; do not clip them into the feasible domain. Report domain violations,
pre-filter geometric validity and the fraction of scenes with at least one valid
candidate. Select the lowest-cost valid candidate with the same exact geometry
checker for all methods. Cost is crouch + .1*yaw + .05*abs(shift). All methods have
32 candidate checks. References: first32 pool motions (retrieval) and uniform32
continuous motions. Record mean selected cost and selection count, not cost alone.
The reference-pool feasible scene count is a finite-pool diagnostic, not proof that
all other scenes are infeasible. An accepted candidate being safe by its oracle
filter is not a learned safety result. No additional test-based tuning or retries.

Interpretation: learned attention must earn its complexity over fixed aggregation
and deterministic interpolation. Flow must improve candidate coverage/quality over
retrieval and uniform sampling to motivate scaling. A negative result narrows the
next question; do not manufacture an attention or FM gain by changing test choices.
