First representation experiment — completed 2026-09-19

The added relation-supervision gradient did not reduce the primary fixed-threshold
false-safe rate in this pilot. It retained more safe candidates, but also accepted
more unsafe ones. This is a useful negative result for the proposed starting recipe.

Scope: 432 procedural box scenes grouped into 144 base families, with 288/72/72
train/validation/test scenes. Each scene uses the same 12 whole-body sphere-proxy
candidates. The test set has 864 scene/candidate pairs: 359 meet a 20 mm clearance
margin and 505 do not. Three nearby geometry variants stay in one family/split.
Labels check linearly swept spheres continuously between knots, not just frames.
No actual human/robot trajectories are used in this training experiment.

Each scene representation contains 32 float32 scalars. Baseline and treatment use
the same networks, initialization, data batches, labels and optimizer steps. The
only pretraining intervention is whether relation loss propagates into the scene
encoder. Frozen encoders are evaluated through freshly initialized common probes.
Three seeds, two trained arms and one random-encoder control completed 7,800 total
optimizer updates. Model selection used the declared final update, never test loss.

| Method | False-safe rate ↓ | Safe-candidate recall ↑ | Clearance MAE ↓ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Random encoder | 21.78 ± 7.56% | 71.59 ± 6.59% | 77.65 ± 1.29 mm |
| Reconstruction encoder | 10.96 ± 4.77% | 77.44 ± 7.20% | 60.52 ± 1.94 mm |
| Reconstruction + relation gradient | 14.98 ± 2.31% | 84.22 ± 1.16% | 59.02 ± 2.39 mm |

Values are mean ± sample SD over three fitted seeds. False-safe rate is unsafe
candidates predicted safe divided by all true unsafe candidates. It includes
collision and insufficient margin; it is not a measured robot collision rate.
The relation arm had higher false-safe rates in all three paired seeds. Its mean
unsafe fraction among accepted candidates was 19.96%, versus 16.18% for reconstruction.
Its safe selection on feasible scenes was 43.33%, versus 53.33% for reconstruction.

The seed-averaged **family-macro** false-safe difference was +5.81 percentage points,
with a paired family-bootstrap 95% interval [+2.54, +9.20]. This has a different
weighting from the pooled-candidate rates in the table. It conditions on these
fitted models and resamples 23 test families with unsafe candidates; it does not
estimate uncertainty across new acquisition corpora or a population of training seeds.

Validation-only threshold calibration produced zero observed test unsafe accepts
for both trained arms, at the cost of retaining only about 12.63% (reconstruction)
and 17.64% (relation) of safe candidates. This is a small, conditional observation,
not a safety guarantee. Random controls abstained completely after calibration.
It does not reverse the primary fixed-threshold result or establish useful navigation.

A post-result bias diagnostic was saved separately. It examines optimistic distance
errors on unsafe candidates without retraining or changing test thresholds. The
results are consistent with a safety/recall tradeoff; they do not isolate whether
the remaining limitation is compression, the finite-capacity probe, optimization,
or the continuous regression objective.

Next discriminating experiment: retain this completed pilot and use a separately
declared experiment to compare boundary classification/uncertainty with a stronger
uncompressed readout and matched coverage. In parallel, qualify real body geometry
from the locally available reference corpus. Only then connect a flow-matching
motion generator and evaluate full execution. Do not scale this recipe on the
assumption that relation supervision has already improved safety.
