Boundary/readout diagnostic v2 — specified before training and test predictions

Question: does the first pilot's safety/recall tradeoff persist at matched
acceptance coverage, and does it change with readout objective or access to the
uncompressed scene grid? This is an adaptive follow-up to v1, not a confirmatory
replication or a new flow-matching policy.

Reuse v1 training and validation scenes and its three fitted reconstruction and
relation encoders. Freeze both encoders. Generate 48 new test scene families
(144 scenes, seed 20260920) with the unchanged procedural distribution and fixed
12-candidate bank. Exclude v1 test scenes. New family identifiers are disjoint.
No test prediction determines model selection, steps, architecture or thresholds.
The small smoke run uses separate data and does not determine hyperparameters.

Cross three inputs (frozen 32-scalar reconstruction encoder, frozen 32-scalar
relation encoder, raw 1400-scalar grid) with three readout objectives:

- MSE on clipped normalized segment clearance, as in v1.
- Binary cross-entropy on whether segment clearance reaches 20 mm.
- Heteroscedastic Gaussian negative log likelihood for the same clipped clearance.
  Score each segment by mean minus 1.645 predicted standard deviations, with
  a 5 mm standard deviation floor. This is a residual-scale heuristic: deterministic
  labels do not supply aleatoric truth, and it is not epistemic uncertainty or a
  calibrated 95% guarantee. Clipping and boundary-biased sampling also affect it.

Use identical two-output query MLPs within each input, 128 hidden units, matching
seeds/initialization and identical sampled training queries. MSE/BCE use the first
output; Gaussian uses both. All three objectives get the same label information.
Use 600 updates, final checkpoint, Adam 0.001, 24 scenes x 48 queries per update,
half uniform and half within 80 mm of the boundary. BCE probabilities are not
claimed calibrated under this sampling. Candidate score is the minimum segment
score; minimum logits are ranking scores, not joint candidate probabilities.

Raw-grid access removes the explicit learned 32-scalar bottleneck but increases
first-layer parameters and computation. It is a diagnostic comparator, not an
isolated causal estimate of bottleneck capacity or an optimized upper bound.
Record parameter counts. Keep analytic geometry oracle and deterministic local
trilinear-grid readout as references, not learned competitors. The latter samples
17 points per segment, subtracts sphere radius, and has interpolation/sampling
error; neither reference establishes full robot or dynamics safety.

Primary: unsafe fraction among exactly the top floor(0.30*N) candidate scores.
Report counts, safe recall, actual collisions and low-cost selection on feasible
scenes. Secondary fixed coverages: 10%, 20%, 40%, 50%. Stable flattened candidate
order breaks score ties without using labels. Ranking on test scores is a
retrospective discrimination diagnostic, not a deployable test-fitted threshold.
Separately select a 30%-coverage score threshold using validation scores only and
report actual test coverage and risk. Exact coverage is not promised under shift.

Planned paired contrasts at 30% coverage: raw_grid/mse minus reconstruction/mse;
reconstruction/bce minus reconstruction/mse; reconstruction/gaussian minus
reconstruction/mse; relation/mse minus reconstruction/mse; relation/bce minus
reconstruction/bce. Use 2,000 paired scene-family bootstrap resamples, re-ranking
within each resample, and average paired risk differences over three fitted seeds.
Intervals condition on the models/distribution, do not capture acquisition or seed
population uncertainty, and are not adjusted for multiple exploratory comparisons.

Interpretation: loss-dependent gains weaken the claim that compression alone is
limiting. Raw-grid gains motivate representation work but retain capacity/optimizer
confounds. A good local-grid reference with poor learned models indicates available
geometric information and an inadequate learned readout in this setting. No gain
does not prove information preservation or impossibility. Preserve all outcomes.

Scope remains privileged box geometry with linearly swept sphere proxies, no
observational occlusion, real human trajectories, SONIC execution or flow matching.
Archive config, source, dataset and upstream checkpoint hashes before each run.
